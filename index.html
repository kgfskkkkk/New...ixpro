Getting started

Three steps to pairing

Pairing links your real WhatsApp number to the bot session — no separate account needed.

1

Open the pair page

Head to the pairing screen and enter your WhatsApp number with the country code.

2

Get your code

A one-time pairing code is generated for that number — no QR scanning required.

3

Link on WhatsApp

Enter the code under WhatsApp → Linked Devices, and the bot goes live on your number.
